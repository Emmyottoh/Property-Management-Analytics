# 🏠 Property Management & House Rent Analytics Suite

## 📌 Project Overview
Navigating dynamic real estate markets requires property managers and real estate investors to analyze rental valuations, tenant preference distributions, and area-type price variations.

This project delivers an interactive executive performance suite evaluating **4,745 property listings** with an **Overall Average Rent of $34,998**. It provides deep operational visibility into rent escalation trends, bedroom-hall-kitchen (BHK) configurations, tenant classifications, and city-level pricing dynamics.

---

## 🎯 Key Business & Market Objectives
* **Rent Price Escalation Mapping:** Identify pricing trends across property sizes and configuration levels to set competitive lease rates.
* **BHK & Area Performance Audit:** Evaluate average rent rates across 1 to 6 BHK units and area types (BHK Area, Carpet Area, Super Area).
* **Tenant Preference Segmentation:** Analyze property share across tenant categories (Bachelors, Family, Bachelors/Family) to optimize leasing strategy.
* **Geographic Market Comparison:** Compare rental yield variations across major metropolitan hubs (Bangalore, Chennai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Mumbai).

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Quality Control
* **Data Auditing:** Processed 4,745 raw property records in Excel and Power Query to address missing values and inconsistent formatting.
* **Field Standardization:** Standardized locality descriptions, area measurements, and furnishing status labels (Furnished, Semi-Furnished, Unfurnished).
* **Metric Validation:** Verified statistical averages for rent rates and square footage across all city segments to eliminate extreme outliers.

---

## 💡 Key Market Insights
* **Configuration Impact:** Rent prices escalate sharply as BHK count increases, with 5 and 6 BHK units demonstrating the highest margin yield.
* **Tenant Distribution:** Properties targeting both Bachelors and Families represent the largest share of market occupancy.
* **Furnishing Premium:** Semi-furnished and fully-furnished listings command significantly higher average rents compared to unfurnished units across all tier-1 cities.

---
